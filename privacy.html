1. Overview

IslamicSchoolReview.com is operated by Rfala LLC. This policy applies to the website and describes information processed when you use it.

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form or email info@islamicschoolreview.com.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored hashed), optional profile details (city, role: parent, educator, etc.).
  • Reviews: the rating, title, text, and account details associated with a review you submit.
  • Forms: the contact details and information you provide in contact, school-submission, claim, or mailing-list forms.
  • School information: information submitted to propose or correct a school listing.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Request data: hosting and database providers may process technical request information needed to deliver the site and its features.
  • Browser storage: the sign-in session is stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Third-party requests: loading Google Fonts may share your IP address and browser request data with Google. The homepage prayer-times widget sends its configured city, state, and country (currently Jacksonville, FL, US) to the Aladhan API to retrieve prayer times.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • School data: school listing details may be provided by schools or drawn from publicly available information.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only in these specific cases:

  • Service providers. Vercel hosts the website, Supabase provides authentication and database services, Formspree processes form submissions, Google Fonts serves typefaces, and the Aladhan API supplies prayer times for the configured city.
  • Publicly. Reviews and profile details selected for publication are visible on the website.
  • When required. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law.

We do not currently use automated machine-learning or sentiment-analysis processing on review content.

5. Cookies and tracking

The sign-in flow stores its session in browser local storage. The site does not currently use a cookie banner or load an analytics script.

  • Authentication: the browser stores a session token in local storage to keep you signed in.

6. How long we keep data

  • Accounts are kept until the account is deleted.
  • Reviews are kept while published.
  • Form submissions are kept for 12 months, then deleted.

7. Security

Authentication and database services are provided by Supabase, and the website is hosted by Vercel. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your rights

You may contact us to ask about personal information submitted through the site or request a correction or deletion. Use the contact form or email info@islamicschoolreview.com. We aim to reply within 3 business days. Applicable rights depend on your location and the operating entity.

9. Children's privacy

The site is not designed to collect information directly from children under 13. You must be 18 or older to create an account.

10. International data transfers

Service providers may process information in the United States and other locations where they operate.
